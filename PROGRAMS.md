## 💰 Scholarships & fellowships

**Rolling / no deadline — apply anytime:**
- **Z Fellows** ($10k, 1-week SF program) · **Emergent Ventures** (grant)
- **1517 Medici** ($1k+) · **Telora** ($10k, under 24)
- **Afore Grants** ($1k) — ⚠️ age-gated at ≤21, check eligibility first
- **Thiel Fellowship** ($100k, requires dropping out — listed for completeness)

Several of these fund *building something you already have* — RISEE Finance and
Volee are both real shipped products, which is exactly what Z Fellows, Emergent
Ventures and 1517 want to see. That's a better use of a second-year fall than
most scholarship essays.

**Fall 2026 calendar:**
- ~Aug–Sep: **Neo Scholars** cohort · **Contrary Venture Partners** apps ·
  **KP Fellows** · **8VC Fellowship**
- Sep 16: **UPE/ACM award** · Sep 30: **IEEE CS Merwin**
- Oct 31: **UPE Scholarship**
- Jan 1: **O'Shaughnessy Fellowships** reopen ($100k, equity-free)
- ~Mar 30: **Astronaut Scholarship** — GT is a partner school; needs a faculty
  nomination, so line one up in the fall. Sophomores and juniors eligible.
- **AFCEA** STEM Majors / NOVA / Belvoir Kevin Carroll

**Later-year flags (junior year = 2027–28, senior = 2028–29):**
- **Goldwater** — junior-year application; GT internal deadline ~Dec. It needs a
  research narrative, which is the long pole, which is why PURA now matters.
- **HKN Beta Mu** induction (CoC juniors, GPA-gated) → unlocks the Madni award
- **Churchill** (Cambridge Part III), **Hertz**, **Marshall**, **Rhodes**,
  **Phi Kappa Phi** — all senior-year, so fall 2028
- **CRA Outstanding Undergraduate Researcher** — needs a research record
